\noindent\textbf{Authority:}

This Governance Charter has been officially adopted by the NCE IT Club under the joint attestation of President Shlok Pandey and Er.~Suroj Burlakoti, Head of the Department of Electronics and Computer Engineering. It builds upon the initial framework (Interim Draft IV) prepared during the tenure of President Prayash Niraula on Jestha 24, 2082 B.S.

\vspace{0.8cm}

\noindent\textbf{Scope:}

This document establishes the organizational structure, operational procedures, membership requirements, and governing rules of the NCE IT Club. It defines the rights and responsibilities of all members, the authority and duties of elected officers, the processes for decision-making and governance, and the standards of conduct expected of all participants. It serves as the primary framework for all club activities and operations.

\vspace{0.8cm}

\noindent\textbf{Applicability:}

All provisions of this Governance Charter are binding upon all members, officers, and representatives of the NCE IT Club. This document applies to all activities conducted under the name or authority of the Club, whether held on or off campus. All individuals who hold membership in the Club, whether active or in any other capacity, are required to adhere to the principles and procedures outlined herein. The Club reserves the right to interpret and enforce these provisions in accordance with its governance authority.
