% ============================================
% ARTICLE II: MEMBERSHIP
% ============================================
\article{Membership}

\gsection{Target Membership}

\begin{enumerate}[label=\alph*), leftmargin=2em, itemsep=0.3em]
    \item The Club shall aim to maintain a maximum of 15 active members.
    \item An odd number of members is preferred to facilitate clear outcomes in voting.
    \item The Executive Board is responsible for managing recruitment to maintain a membership level that ensures operational effectiveness and balanced representation across year groups.
\end{enumerate}

\gsection{Eligibility}

\textbf{General Requirements:}
\begin{enumerate}[label=\alph*), leftmargin=2em, itemsep=0.3em]
    \item Membership is open only to students enrolled in the Bachelor of Computer Engineering (BCT) or Bachelor in Electronics, Communication and Information Engineering (BEI) programs at NCE.
    \item Students in their first, second, or third year may apply for membership.
    \item Fourth year students cannot apply for new membership. However, current members who reach fourth year may remain members for the duration of their tenure.
    \item All applicants must be in good academic and disciplinary standing with the college.
\end{enumerate}
\\
\textbf{Recruitment Discretion:}
The Executive Board may, at its discretion, limit recruitment to specific year groups to ensure balanced representation and long-term continuity of the Club.

\gsection{Enrollment Process}

New members are selected through the following structured process:
\\
\textbf{Step 1 -- Application:}
\begin{enumerate}[label=\alph*), leftmargin=2em, itemsep=0.3em]
    \item Candidates complete an online application form (Google Form) during announced recruitment periods.
    \item The application form shall include, at minimum:
    \begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em, itemsep=0.2em]
        \item Full name, year of study, and academic program (BCT or BEI)
        \item Contact information (Gmail address and phone number)
        \item Responses designed to assess the five evaluation criteria specified in this Section
        \item Any prior experience or collaborative works
        \item Statement of interest and commitment to Club activities
    \end{itemize}
    \item The form questions shall be designed to directly align with the evaluation criteria to ensure fair and transparent assessment.
\end{enumerate}
\\
\textbf{Step 2 -- Interview:}
\begin{enumerate}[label=\alph*), leftmargin=2em, itemsep=0.3em]
    \item Shortlisted candidates attend an in-person interview with the President and the full Executive Board present.
    \item The President shall lead the interview process and may assign specific questions to other Executive Board members.
    \item Any Club member present during the interview may ask questions of the candidate and provide feedback to the Executive Board. However, only the Executive Board members shall assign numerical scores.
    \item Members' observations and suggestions shall be documented and considered by the Executive Board during evaluation.
\end{enumerate}
\\
\textbf{Step 3 -- Evaluation:}
\begin{enumerate}[label=\alph*), leftmargin=2em, itemsep=0.3em]
    \item Candidates are assessed on the following five criteria, each carrying a maximum of 10 marks, for a total of 50 marks:
    \begin{enumerate}[label=\arabic*. , leftmargin=2em, itemsep=0.2em]
        \item \textbf{Form Quality (10 marks):} The completeness, clarity, and professionalism of the submitted application.
        \item \textbf{Technical Interest and Foundational Knowledge (10 marks):} Demonstrated enthusiasm for technology, awareness of current trends, and basic technical understanding.
        \item \textbf{Communication Skills (10 marks):} Ability to articulate ideas clearly, active listening, and professional demeanor during the interview.
        \item \textbf{Commitment to Club Activities (10 marks):} Willingness to dedicate time, participate actively, and contribute to Club goals.
        \item \textbf{Prior Experience and Collaborative Works (10 marks):} Any relevant projects, teamwork experience, or demonstrated ability to work collaboratively.
    \end{enumerate}
    \item Scores shall be recorded by the Secretary and maintained in a spreadsheet format (MS Excel or equivalent) and made available to the Executive Board for final selection.
\end{enumerate}
\\
\textbf{Step 4 -- Selection:}
\begin{enumerate}[label=\alph*), leftmargin=2em, itemsep=0.3em]
    \item The Executive Board shall complete the selection process within 24 hours of the conclusion of all interviews.
    \item Successful candidates shall receive email notification of their acceptance.
    \item Newly selected members shall be granted access to all Club communication channels (including group chats, working Gmail, and shared drives) within one week of selection.
    \item New members shall attend an orientation session organized by the Executive Board within two weeks of selection.
\end{enumerate}

\gsection{Member Rights}

All members have the right to:
\begin{enumerate}[label=\alph*), leftmargin=2em, itemsep=0.3em]
    \item Attend all Club meetings and participate in discussions.
    \item Vote on Club matters when voting is called.
    \item Participate in all Club events and activities without payment of entry fees.
    \item Propose ideas, projects, or suggestions for Club improvement.
    \item Access Club resources and facilities (Club Room, accounts, and digital assets as authorized).
    \item Apply for officer positions when eligible.
    \item Request to use Club funds for Club-related expenses, subject to the procedures outlined in Article V.
    \item Review Club financial records in accordance with Article V, Section 6.
\end{enumerate}
\\
\textbf{Club Fund Usage by Members:}
\begin{enumerate}[label=\alph*), leftmargin=2em, itemsep=0.3em]
    \item Members may use Club funds for Club-related expenses when authorized.
    \item Members who are not part of the Executive duo (President and Secretary) must obtain prior approval from the Treasurer or President before incurring expenses on behalf of the Club.
    \item The Executive duo (President and Secretary) may incur Club-related expenses directly and shall seek reimbursement from the Treasurer by submitting receipts.
    \item All Club fund expenditures must be documented with receipts and logged in the Club's financial statement.
    \item The Treasurer maintains sole authority over the Club's financial account and shall process reimbursements within 7 days of receiving valid documentation.
\end{enumerate}

\gsection{Member Responsibilities}

\textbf{Core Responsibilities:}
All members are expected to:
\begin{enumerate}[label=\alph*), leftmargin=2em, itemsep=0.3em]
    \item Uphold the Club's mission and values.
    \item Actively participate in Club activities and meetings.
    \item Adhere to the Code of Conduct (Article VII).
    \item Fulfill assigned responsibilities in a timely manner.
    \item Represent the Club in a professional and positive manner.
    \item Maintain the confidentiality of internal Club matters.
\end{enumerate}
\\
\textbf{Operational Support Roles:}
\begin{enumerate}[label=\alph*), leftmargin=2em, itemsep=0.3em]
    \item In addition to their core responsibilities, all members shall contribute to the Club's operational needs.
    \item These duties are assigned on a rotating basis by the Executive Board to ensure equitable participation.
    \item Responsibilities include, but are not limited to:
    \begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em, itemsep=0.2em]
        \item \textbf{Administrative Support:} Assisting with the creation of digital forms for event registration and feedback collection.
        \item \textbf{Event Support:} Serving as hosts or facilitators during events, and assisting with setup, coordination, and cleanup.
        \item \textbf{Logistical Coordination:} Assisting in the procurement and management of event materials and equipment, including collecting approved items from the college administration.
        \item \textbf{Content Preparation:} Assisting in the creation of presentation materials or other digital content for Club activities.
    \end{itemize}
    \item These roles are vital for the successful execution of Club activities and provide all members with opportunities to develop practical skills.
\end{enumerate}
\\
\textbf{Event Participation:}
\begin{enumerate}[label=\alph*), leftmargin=2em, itemsep=0.3em]
    \item Members are expected to be present and contribute to the successful execution of Club events.
    \item For multi-day events, responsibilities may be rotated, with coordination managed by the Event Manager.
    \item All members are expected to be present on the final day of an event unless they have received prior approval for their absence from the President.
    \item Any member unable to attend must provide advance notice to the Event Manager with a valid reason.
\end{enumerate}
\\
\textbf{Club Room Conduct:}
\begin{enumerate}[label=\alph*), leftmargin=2em, itemsep=0.3em]
    \item The Club Room and its contents are the property of the Department and must be treated with respect.
    \item All members are collectively responsible for maintaining a clean and organized environment in the Club Room.
    \item Members shall adhere to all key management rules specified in Article I, Section 7.
\end{enumerate}

\gsection{Voluntary Resignation}

\begin{enumerate}[label=\alph*), leftmargin=2em, itemsep=0.3em]
    \item A member may not voluntarily leave the Club without formal notice.
    \item Any member wishing to resign must submit a formal written application or official email to the President at least one week (7 days) in advance of their intended departure date.
    \item The resignation shall be formally acknowledged by the President or Secretary.
    \item A resigning member must complete the Departure Protocol (Section 8) before their resignation is considered complete.
    \item Members who ``ghost'' the Club (cease participation without formal notice) for a period of 30 days shall be considered for involuntary membership review under Section 7.
\end{enumerate}

\gsection{Membership Termination}

\textbf{Grounds for Termination:}
Membership may be terminated for:
\begin{enumerate}[label=\alph*), leftmargin=2em, itemsep=0.3em]
    \item Repeated violations of the Code of Conduct.
    \item Failure to fulfill member responsibilities despite written warnings.
    \item Actions that damage the Club's reputation or standing within the college community.
    \item Misuse of Club resources, accounts, or information.
    \item Extended inactivity without valid reason (30 consecutive days of unexcused absence from all Club activities and meetings).
    \item Unauthorized use of Club accounts, social media, or materials.
    \item Conviction under the college's disciplinary code for offenses that reflect poorly on the Club.
\end{enumerate}
\\
\textbf{Termination Process:}
\begin{enumerate}[label=\alph*), leftmargin=2em, itemsep=0.3em]
    \item A decision to terminate a membership requires a two-thirds (2/3) majority vote of the Executive Board.
    \item The Executive Board may initiate termination proceedings in accordance with the disciplinary procedures outlined in Article VII.
    \item The member in question shall be given a fair opportunity to present their case before the Executive Board before a final decision is made.
    \item The member shall receive written notice of the termination decision, including the specific grounds and the effective date.
    \item A terminated member may appeal the decision to the Head of Department within 14 days.
\end{enumerate}

\gsection{Departure Protocol}

Upon voluntary or involuntary departure from the Club, a member must:
\begin{enumerate}[label=\alph*), leftmargin=2em, itemsep=0.3em]
    \item Return all Club property and resources in their possession, including but not limited to:
    \begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em, itemsep=0.2em]
        \item Any assigned role badges (within 48 hours of departure)
        \item Any Club Room keys or Presidential Desk keys (within 48 hours of departure)
        \item Any Club equipment, materials, or documents
    \end{itemize}
    \item Relinquish access to all official Club accounts and digital assets.
    \item Complete any pending operational duties or formally hand them over to another member.
    \item The Secretary shall formally document the member's departure and update all relevant records within 7 days.
\end{enumerate}

Failure to comply with the Departure Protocol, particularly the return of keys and badges within the 48-hour window, shall result in the fines specified in Article I, Section 8.
