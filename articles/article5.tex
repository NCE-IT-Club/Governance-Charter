\article{Financial Management}

\gsection{Budgeting and Reporting}

\textbf{Event Budgets:}
\begin{enumerate}[label=\alph*), leftmargin=2em, itemsep=0.3em]
    \item The Treasurer shall prepare a preliminary budget for any event involving financial transactions.
    \item This budget must detail all anticipated expenditures including:
    \begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em, itemsep=0.2em]
        \item Printing and stationery costs
        \item Mentor or guest speaker honorariums and refreshments
        \item Participant refreshments (for events exceeding 2 hours)
        \item Equipment or material procurement
        \item Miscellaneous operational expenses
    \end{itemize}
    \item The preliminary budget must be approved by the President before any expenditures are incurred.
    \item The Event Manager shall provide the Treasurer with logistical requirements to inform the budget.
\end{enumerate}
\\
\textbf{Post-Event Financial Reports:}
\begin{enumerate}[label=\alph*), leftmargin=2em, itemsep=0.3em]
    \item Following each event, the Treasurer shall prepare a final financial report detailing actual income and expenditures.
    \item The final report shall compare actual spending against the preliminary budget and explain any variances exceeding 20\%.
    \item Final reports shall be submitted to the President within 7 days of the event's conclusion.
\end{enumerate}
\\
\textbf{Semesterly Financial Reports:}
\begin{enumerate}[label=\alph*), leftmargin=2em, itemsep=0.3em]
    \item The Treasurer shall provide a comprehensive semesterly financial report to the Executive Board.
    \item This report shall be made available to all members within 14 days of the semester's end.
    \item The report must include: opening balance, all income and expenditures with receipts, closing balance, and any outstanding liabilities.
\end{enumerate}

\gsection{Club Fund Usage by Members}

\begin{enumerate}[label=\alph*), leftmargin=2em, itemsep=0.3em]
    \item Members may use Club funds for Club-related expenses under the following conditions:
    
    \item \textbf{Executive Duo (President and Secretary):}
    \begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em, itemsep=0.2em]
        \item May incur Club-related expenses directly without prior approval.
        \item Must retain all receipts and submit them to the Treasurer for reimbursement within 7 days.
        \item Expenses exceeding NPR 2,000 require advance notification to the Treasurer.
    \end{itemize}
    
    \item \textbf{Other Members:}
    \begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em, itemsep=0.2em]
        \item Must obtain prior written or verbal approval from the President or Treasurer before incurring expenses.
        \item Must present a receipt for reimbursement.
        \item Must submit reimbursement requests to the Treasurer within 7 days of the expenditure.
    \end{itemize}
    
    \item \textbf{Reimbursement Processing:}
    \begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em, itemsep=0.2em]
        \item The Treasurer shall process all valid reimbursement requests within 7 days.
        \item Reimbursements shall be recorded in the financial statement at the time of processing.
        \item Members who fail to provide receipts within the 7-day window forfeit their reimbursement claim.
    \end{itemize}
    
    \item \textbf{Prohibited Uses:} Club funds shall not be used for:
    \begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em, itemsep=0.2em]
        \item Personal expenses unrelated to Club activities
        \item Alcohol, tobacco, or any prohibited substances
        \item Activities that violate college policies
        \item Any expenditure not documented with a valid receipt
    \end{itemize}
\end{enumerate}

\gsection{Financial Records}

\begin{enumerate}[label=\alph*), leftmargin=2em, itemsep=0.3em]
    \item The Treasurer is responsible for maintaining accurate and detailed records of all financial transactions.
    
    \item \textbf{Single Statement Document:} The Treasurer shall maintain a single, continuous financial statement document for their entire tenure. This document shall contain:
    \begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em, itemsep=0.2em]
        \item A running ledger of all transactions in chronological order
        \item Date, description, category, amount, and running balance for each entry
        \item All income sources and expenditure categories clearly labeled
        \item Supporting receipt references for each transaction
        \item Monthly summary balances
    \end{itemize}
    
    \item No member shall maintain a separate financial record without authorization from the Executive Board.
    
    \item All financial records must be archived for a minimum of three years.
    
    \item \textbf{Financial Review Access:} These records shall be available for review by any member upon reasonable request. However, members must provide the Treasurer with at least 3 days advance notice to ensure the statement is properly compiled and balanced before viewing.
\end{enumerate}

\gsection{Fundraising}

\begin{enumerate}[label=\alph*), leftmargin=2em, itemsep=0.3em]
    \item All fundraising activities must be approved by the Executive Board and comply with college policies.
    \item Fundraising initiatives may include:
    \begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em, itemsep=0.2em]
        \item Sponsorship requests from local businesses
        \item Collaborative events with other organizations
        \item Registration fees for premium events (with approval)
        \item Merchandise sales
    \end{itemize}
    \item Any fundraising initiatives involving external organizations require prior approval from the Head of Department.
    \item All funds raised shall be deposited into the Club's official financial account within 48 hours of collection.
    \item The Treasurer shall maintain separate records for fundraising income and expenditure.
\end{enumerate}

\gsection{Minimum Operating Fund}

\begin{enumerate}[label=\alph*), leftmargin=2em, itemsep=0.3em]
    \item The Club shall maintain a minimum opening operating fund balance of NPR 1,000 at the beginning of each tenure.
    \item In the event the opening fund at the start of a tenure is below this threshold, a nominal contribution of NPR 100 per member shall be collected to meet the minimum requirement.
    \item The Executive Board may grant exemptions to members facing financial hardship upon written request.
    \item The minimum operating fund threshold may be adjusted by Executive Board decision, subject to member notification, based on changing operational needs.
\end{enumerate}

\gsection{Financial Review Procedures}

\begin{enumerate}[label=\alph*), leftmargin=2em, itemsep=0.3em]
    \item Any member may request to review the Club's financial records by submitting a written request to the Treasurer.
    \item The request must include:
    \begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em, itemsep=0.2em]
        \item The member's name and date of request
        \item The specific records or time period being requested
        \item The purpose of the review
    \end{itemize}
    \item The Treasurer shall provide access to the requested records within 3 business days of receiving the request.
    \item Financial reviews shall be conducted in the presence of the Treasurer or a designated Executive Board member.
    \item Members may not remove, copy, or photograph financial records without written authorization from the President.
\end{enumerate}
