% ============================================
% ARTICLE VII: CODE OF CONDUCT & DISCIPLINARY PROCEDURES
% ============================================
\article{Code of Conduct \& Disciplinary Procedures}

\gsection{General Conduct}

All members are expected to conduct themselves in a manner that upholds the dignity of the Club and the college. The following standards apply:

\begin{enumerate}[label=\alph*), leftmargin=2em, itemsep=0.3em]
    \item \textbf{Integrity and Professionalism:} Members shall conduct themselves with honesty, integrity, and professionalism in all Club-related activities.
    
    \item \textbf{Respectful Environment:} Members shall contribute to a positive, inclusive, and collaborative environment. Disagreements shall be handled constructively.
    
    \item \textbf{Anti-Harassment:} Members shall refrain from any form of harassment, discrimination, bullying, or intimidation. This includes but is not limited to harassment based on gender, ethnicity, religion, academic background, or technical skill level.
    
    \item \textbf{Anti-Plagiarism:} Members shall not present others' work as their own in any Club project, competition, or publication.
    
    \item \textbf{Resource Responsibility:} Members shall use Club resources, including its name, logo, and digital assets, responsibly and solely for official Club purposes.
    
    \item \textbf{Facility Maintenance:} Members shall maintain the cleanliness and order of the Club Room and its equipment.
    
    \item \textbf{College Policy Compliance:} Members shall adhere to all applicable rules, regulations, and codes of conduct established by the National College of Engineering and the Department of Electronics and Computer Engineering. In case of conflict between this document and college regulations, college regulations shall take precedence, and this document shall be amended to achieve alignment.
    
    \item \textbf{Balanced Environment:} While the Club maintains professional standards, it also values camaraderie and mutual support. Light-hearted interaction, friendly debate, and humor are encouraged, provided they remain respectful and do not cross into harassment, mockery, or exclusion.
    
    \item \textbf{Intellectual Openness:} Members may engage in discussions on diverse topics including political, social, and technological issues, provided all discussions remain respectful and do not disrupt Club operations or create a hostile environment.
\end{enumerate}

\gsection{Digital Conduct}

\begin{enumerate}[label=\alph*), leftmargin=2em, itemsep=0.3em]
    \item Members shall maintain professional conduct in all official Club communication channels, including but not limited to WhatsApp groups, Messenger groups, email threads, and shared digital workspaces.
    
    \item The following behaviors are strictly prohibited in official communication channels:
    \begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em, itemsep=0.2em]
        \item ``Shitposting,'' spamming, or flooding channels with irrelevant content
        \item Toxic behavior, personal attacks, or inflammatory language
        \item Sharing of confidential Club information with non-members
        \item Using official channels for personal promotion or non-Club business
        \item Unauthorized addition of non-members to official groups
    \end{itemize}
    
    \item Members found violating digital conduct rules shall be subject to the disciplinary process outlined in Section 4 of this Article.
    
    \item The President and Secretary reserve the right to remove any message or content that violates these standards.
\end{enumerate}

\gsection{Meeting Focus Rules}

\begin{enumerate}[label=\alph*), leftmargin=2em, itemsep=0.3em]
    \item During official Club meetings, members must remain focused on the meeting agenda.
    
    \item Personal assignments, laptops, smartphones, and unrelated electronics must remain closed unless:
    \begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em, itemsep=0.2em]
        \item The President explicitly designates the meeting as a ``Working Session''
        \item A member is using the device to present or demonstrate Club-related content
        \item An emergency situation requires immediate attention
    \end{itemize}
    
    \item Members who repeatedly distract others or engage in off-topic activities during meetings may be subject to verbal warning.
    
    \item The President may designate specific meetings as ``Working Sessions'' where members are expected to use their devices for collaborative work.
\end{enumerate}

\gsection{Disciplinary Measures}

Violations of the Code of Conduct shall be addressed through a progressive four-stage disciplinary process:
\\
\textbf{Stage 1 -- Verbal Warning:}
\begin{enumerate}[label=\alph*), leftmargin=2em, itemsep=0.3em]
    \item An informal but documented discussion between the offending member and the President or a designated Executive Board member.
    \item The member shall be informed of the specific violation and the expected correction.
    \item The warning shall be documented in the member's performance record.
    \item This stage is triggered automatically upon:
    \begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em, itemsep=0.2em]
        \item Missing two consecutive scheduled meetings without approved reason (Article IV, Section 6)
        \item First instance of minor digital conduct violation
        \item First instance of meeting focus rule violation
    \end{itemize}
\end{enumerate}
\\
\textbf{Stage 2 -- Written Warning:}
\begin{enumerate}[label=\alph*), leftmargin=2em, itemsep=0.3em]
    \item A formal, documented warning issued by the Executive Board.
    \item The written warning shall specify:
    \begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em, itemsep=0.2em]
        \item The nature of the violation
        \item Previous warnings (if any)
        \item Expected behavioral changes
        \item Consequences of further violations
    \end{itemize}
    \item The warning shall be signed by the President and Secretary and copied to the member.
    \item The member shall acknowledge receipt of the warning in writing.
    \item This stage is triggered by:
    \begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em, itemsep=0.2em]
        \item Repeated violation after a Stage 1 warning
        \item First instance of moderate misconduct (misuse of Club resources, unauthorized sharing of Club assets)
        \item Failure to pay asset fines within the 14-day window (Article I, Section 8)
    \end{itemize}
\end{enumerate}
\\
\textbf{Stage 3 -- Temporary Suspension:}
\begin{enumerate}[label=\alph*), leftmargin=2em, itemsep=0.3em]
    \item Suspension constitutes a temporary, complete restriction of Club privileges for a designated timeframe.
    
    \item During suspension, the member shall:
    \begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em, itemsep=0.2em]
        \item Be banned from the physical Club Room for the duration of the suspension
        \item Be temporarily removed from all official WhatsApp and Messenger communication groups
        \item Be barred from representing the Club at any college or external event
        \item Surrender their designated officer badge (if applicable) to the Secretary
        \item Be prohibited from using Club digital assets, accounts, or resources
    \end{itemize}
    
    \item The standard suspension period is 2 weeks (14 days), though the Executive Board may extend this to a maximum of 4 weeks for severe violations.
    
    \item Suspension acts as a final warning before potential Stage 4 termination.
    
    \item Suspended members may appeal the decision to the Head of Department within 7 days of notification.
    
    \item This stage is triggered by:
    \begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em, itemsep=0.2em]
        \item Repeated violation after a Stage 2 warning
        \item Serious misconduct including theft, vandalism, or severe harassment
        \item Unauthorized use of Club accounts for personal purposes
        \item Actions that significantly damage the Club's reputation
    \end{itemize}
\end{enumerate}
\\
\textbf{Stage 4 -- Termination:}
\begin{enumerate}[label=\alph*), leftmargin=2em, itemsep=0.3em]
    \item Permanent removal from the Club.
    \item The member must complete the Departure Protocol (Article II, Section 8).
    \item This stage is triggered by:
    \begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em, itemsep=0.2em]
        \item Repeated violation after a Stage 3 suspension
        \item Severe misconduct warranting immediate termination (vote of 2/3 of Executive Board)
        \item Actions that cause irreparable harm to the Club or its standing
    \end{itemize}
    \item The termination process follows Article II, Section 7.
\end{enumerate}

\gsection{Disciplinary Procedure}

\begin{enumerate}[label=\alph*), leftmargin=2em, itemsep=0.3em]
    \item The Executive Board is responsible for investigating and acting on all disciplinary matters.
    
    \item \textbf{Investigation:}
    \begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em, itemsep=0.2em]
        \item The Executive Board shall gather evidence and witness statements before making a determination.
        \item The member in question shall be informed of the allegations against them.
    \end{itemize}
    
    \item \textbf{Right to be Heard:}
    \begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em, itemsep=0.2em]
        \item The member in question will be given a fair opportunity to present their case before the Executive Board.
        \item For Stage 3 and Stage 4 actions, the member shall receive at least 48 hours notice before the disciplinary hearing.
    \end{itemize}
    
    \item \textbf{Immediate Suspension:} Serious violations may warrant immediate suspension (Stage 3) pending a full investigation. This decision requires unanimous consent of the Executive Board.
    
    \item \textbf{Documentation:} All disciplinary actions shall be documented by the Secretary and kept confidential. Records shall include:
    \begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em, itemsep=0.2em]
        \item Date and nature of the violation
        \item Stage of disciplinary action taken
        \item Member's response (if provided)
        \item Executive Board's decision and rationale
        \item Any conditions for reinstatement or improvement
    \end{itemize}
    
    \item \textbf{Confidentiality:} All disciplinary proceedings and records shall be kept strictly confidential. Discussion of disciplinary matters with non-members or on social media is prohibited and may result in additional disciplinary action.
    
    \item \textbf{Right to Appeal:} Members may appeal disciplinary decisions to the Head of Department as follows:
    \begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em, itemsep=0.2em]
        \item Stage 1 and 2: Appeal within 14 days
        \item Stage 3: Appeal within 7 days
        \item Stage 4: Appeal within 7 days
    \end{itemize}
\end{enumerate}

\gsection{Asset Protection Fines}

To protect physical Club property from neglect or loss, the following fines apply as referenced in Article I, Section 8:

\begin{enumerate}[label=\alph*), leftmargin=2em, itemsep=0.3em]
    \item \textbf{Lost Dedicated Role Badge:} NPR 300 fine, covering re-printing and administrative costs.
    
    \item \textbf{Lost Room Key or Presidential Desk Key:} NPR 500 fine, covering lock replacement and security measures.
    
    \item All fines must be paid within 14 days of written notice from the Treasurer or Secretary.
    
    \item Unpaid fines shall be recorded in the member's performance record and shall escalate to Stage 2 (Written Warning) if not resolved within the 14-day period.
    
    \item Members may dispute a fine by submitting a written appeal to the Executive Board within 7 days of the fine notice. The Executive Board's decision on the appeal shall be final.
\end{enumerate}
