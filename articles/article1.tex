% ============================================
% ARTICLE I: CLUB IDENTITY, PURPOSE, ASSETS & FACILITIES
% ============================================
\article{Club Identity, Purpose, Assets \& Facilities}

\gsection{Official Name}

The official name of this organization is the ``NCE IT Club'' (referred to as ``the Club'' in this document).

\gsection{Purpose}

The Club exists to:
\begin{enumerate}[label=\alph*), leftmargin=2em, itemsep=0.3em]
    \item Promote technical education and skill development in Information Technology.
    \item Provide a platform for students to learn, collaborate, and work on projects.
    \item Organize events, workshops, and activities that benefit students.
    \item Connect students with industry professionals and alumni.
    \item Build a strong community of technology enthusiasts at NCE.
\end{enumerate}

\gsection{Institutional Affiliation}

The Club operates under the Department of Electronics and Computer Engineering at the National College of Engineering. While the Club maintains independence in running its day-to-day activities, it follows college policies and works with departmental guidance.

\gsection{Official Identity Assets}

The Club maintains official identity assets that represent its institutional standing:
\begin{enumerate}[label=\alph*), leftmargin=2em, itemsep=0.3em]
    \item \textbf{Official Seal:} The Club's official seal is maintained by the President and is used only on formal documents, official correspondence, and attested records.
    \item \textbf{Logo:} The Club logo is the sole property of the NCE IT Club. It shall not be used for personal branding, external communication, or unofficial purposes without written approval from the President.
    \item \textbf{Design Templates:} All official design templates (including but not limited to Canva templates, presentation slides, certificate formats, and promotional materials) are Club property and shall not be shared with non-members or used for non-Club activities.
    \item \textbf{Club Stamp:} The physical Club stamp is maintained by the Secretary and shall be used only for officially approved documents.
    \item \textbf{Identity Badge:} Each designated officer shall receive an official role badge. These badges remain Club property and must be returned upon departure.
\end{enumerate}

\gsection{Digital Communication Infrastructure}

The Club maintains a structured digital communication system:
\begin{enumerate}[label=\alph*), leftmargin=2em, itemsep=0.3em]
    \item \textbf{Official Communication Email (Outlook Account with College Domain):} This account is handled strictly by the President, Secretary, and Public Relations Officer for mass mailings, formal administration, and official correspondence with the college and external entities.
    \item \textbf{Working Shared Archive (Primary Gmail):} Dedicated to open internal collaboration, including Canva access, asset storage, and event folder management. Accessible to all active members.
    \item \textbf{Secondary Archive Gmail:} Maintained as a backup by the President and Secretary to prevent digital crowding and ensure continuity of access.
    \item \textbf{Social Media Accounts:} Managed by the Social Media Manager with administrative access held by the President and Secretary.
\end{enumerate}

\gsection{Club Assets --- Aspirational and Protective Provisions}

The Club seeks to acquire and maintain technology assets to support its mission. The following provisions apply to all assets, whether currently held or sought in the future:
\begin{enumerate}[label=\alph*), leftmargin=2em, itemsep=0.3em]
    \item \textbf{Aspirational Assets:} The Club may pursue the acquisition of dedicated computing equipment (such as a workstation PC), software licenses, and technology subscriptions (including but not limited to design tools, development environments, and AI-assisted platforms) through formal request to the Department and College Administration.
    \item \textbf{Asset Request Protocol:} All requests for institutional assets shall be submitted by the President through the Head of Department with a written justification demonstrating how the asset serves the Club's educational mission.
    \item \textbf{Shared Use Principle:} All Club assets are for Club purposes only. Members may not share access credentials, software licenses, or equipment with non-members under any circumstance.
    \item \textbf{Usage Monitoring:} The Executive Board shall monitor asset usage to ensure compliance with licensing terms and institutional policies.
    \item \textbf{Damage Liability:} Members found responsible for damage to Club assets through negligence or misuse shall be subject to disciplinary action under Article VII and may be held financially liable for repair or replacement costs.
\end{enumerate}

\gsection{Club Room and Facilities}

The Club has its own dedicated room provided by the Department of Electronics and Computer Engineering, which serves as the workspace and meeting location.
\\
\textbf{Room Exclusivity:}
\begin{enumerate}[label=\alph*), leftmargin=2em, itemsep=0.3em]
    \item The Club Room is strictly for active NCE IT Club members.
    \item Non-members may not use the Club Room as a casual study space or meeting area.
    \item If outside student groups require the room for academic or collaborative projects, they must submit a written request to the President for permission at least 48 hours in advance.
\end{enumerate}
\\
\textbf{Presidential Workstation:}
\begin{enumerate}[label=\alph*), leftmargin=2em, itemsep=0.3em]
    \item The President's desk, locked drawers, and compartments are reserved exclusively for the President.
    \item No member may sit at the President's desk or access its compartments without direct, explicit permission from the President.
    \item The President's workstation shall house sensitive documents, the Club stamp, and any high-value Club assets.
\end{enumerate}
\\
\textbf{Key Management:}
\begin{enumerate}[label=\alph*), leftmargin=2em, itemsep=0.3em]
    \item The primary keys to the Club Room and the President's desk are held by the President.
    \item A duplicate emergency set is officially assigned to the Secretary.
    \item No additional copies shall be made without written authorization from the Executive Board.
\end{enumerate}
\\
\textbf{Room Etiquette:}
\begin{enumerate}[label=\alph*), leftmargin=2em, itemsep=0.3em]
    \item All members are collectively responsible for maintaining a clean and organized environment in the Club Room.
    \item Sleeping in the Club Room is strictly prohibited.
    \item Hosting external personal study groups or non-Club activities inside the facility is forbidden.
    \item Members' personal belongings (including laptops and bags) may be stored in the Club Room at their own risk; the Club assumes no liability for loss or damage to personal items.
\end{enumerate}

\gsection{Asset Protection and Fines}

To protect physical Club property from neglect or loss, the following fines shall apply:
\begin{enumerate}[label=\alph*), leftmargin=2em, itemsep=0.3em]
    \item \textbf{Lost Dedicated Role Badge:} A fine of NPR 300, covering re-printing and administrative costs.
    \item \textbf{Lost Room Key or Presidential Desk Key:} A fine of NPR 500, covering lock replacement and security measures.
    \item All fines collected shall be deposited directly into the Club operating fund.
    \item Fines must be paid within 14 days of notice. Failure to pay may result in escalation to Stage 2 (Written Warning) under Article VII.
\end{enumerate}
