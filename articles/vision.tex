\section*{Vision}
\sectionmark{Vision}

\textit{Why the Club exists and what we aspire to become}

\vspace{0.5cm}

The vision of the NCE IT Club defines our long term purpose, why we exist and what we hope to achieve over the years.

\subsection*{Why We Exist}

The NCE IT Club exists to:

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=2em, itemsep=0.3em]
    \item Fill the gap between classroom theory and real world practice
    \item Give students opportunities to learn technologies not covered in regular courses
    \item Create a community where tech enthusiasts can connect and grow
    \item Help students become job ready or prepared for their own ventures
    \item Make technology education more practical and accessible
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{What We Aspire to Become}

\subsubsection*{A Recognized Leader}

We aim to be recognized as the leading student technology organization at NCE and gain respect from other colleges. Our reputation should be built on:

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=2em, itemsep=0.3em]
    \item Consistently organizing high quality events
    \item Completing meaningful projects
    \item Developing skilled, professional members
    \item Making real contributions to our college community
\end{itemize}

\subsubsection*{A Hub for Innovation}

We want to create an environment where:

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=2em, itemsep=0.3em]
    \item Members feel free to experiment with new ideas
    \item Students can work on innovative projects
    \item Creative problem solving is encouraged
    \item Technology is used to solve real challenges
\end{itemize}

\subsubsection*{A Career Launchpad}

We aspire to be known as a place that:

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=2em, itemsep=0.3em]
    \item Prepares students for IT careers
    \item Provides practical skills employers value
    \item Offers experience that builds strong resumes
    \item Helps members become confident professionals
    \item Supports those who want to start their own tech ventures
\end{itemize}

\subsubsection*{A Force for Good}

We hope to:

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=2em, itemsep=0.3em]
    \item Make positive contributions to our college and community
    \item Use technology to address real problems
    \item Promote digital literacy and tech education
    \item Support local businesses or organizations through our projects
    \item Build a culture of giving back
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{Our Long Term Commitment}

Over the years, the NCE IT Club should grow stronger, maintain its standards, and continue to provide value to NCE students. Even as members graduate and new ones join, our core purpose remains: to be a place where students passionate about technology can learn, create, collaborate, and prepare for their futures.
