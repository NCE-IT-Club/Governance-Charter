\article{Elections, Transitions \& Governance Authority}

\gsection{Annual Elections}

The Club shall hold elections annually to select a new Executive Board. This Reformation Process ensures leadership continuity and provides opportunities for members to assume greater responsibility.

\begin{enumerate}[label=\alph*), leftmargin=2em, itemsep=0.3em]
    \item Only the Executive Board positions (President, Secretary, Treasurer) shall be filled through democratic election.
    
    \item Designated officer roles (Event Manager, Social Media Manager, Design Lead, Public Relations Officer, Project Lead) shall be appointed by the incoming Executive Board within 48 hours of the election, as specified in Article III.
    
    \item The election shall be conducted as part of the Reformation Meeting.
\end{enumerate}

\gsection{Election Timeline}

\begin{enumerate}[label=\alph*), leftmargin=2em, itemsep=0.3em]
    \item Elections shall be held in the final month of the academic year, prior to the start of final examinations.
    
    \item The Executive Board shall announce the election schedule at least one week (7 days) in advance.
    
    \item The schedule shall include:
    \begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em, itemsep=0.2em]
        \item Nomination period (minimum 3 days)
        \item Campaign period (if applicable)
        \item Election date and time
        \item Results announcement date
        \item Transition period dates
    \end{itemize}
    
    \item If an election cannot be held in the final month due to extraordinary circumstances, the incumbent Executive Board shall continue in a caretaker capacity until an election can be convened, not exceeding 30 days beyond the normal timeline.
\end{enumerate}

\gsection{Eligibility for Office}

\begin{enumerate}[label=\alph*), leftmargin=2em, itemsep=0.3em]
    \item \textbf{President:} Candidates must have completed at least two years of active membership in the NCE IT Club.
    
    \item \textbf{Secretary and Treasurer:} Candidates must have completed at least one year of active membership in the NCE IT Club.
    
    \item All candidates must be members in good standing at the time of the election. ``Good standing'' means:
    \begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em, itemsep=0.2em]
        \item No active disciplinary warnings (Stage 2 or higher)
        \item Attendance rate of at least 70\% in the current academic year
        \item All Club property returned and fines paid
    \end{itemize}
    
    \item A member may not hold more than one Executive Board position simultaneously.
\end{enumerate}

\gsection{Election Procedure}
\\
\textbf{Nominations:}
\begin{enumerate}[label=\alph*), leftmargin=2em, itemsep=0.3em]
    \item Members may nominate themselves or others by submitting a written nomination to the Secretary during the nomination period.
    \item Each nomination must include:
    \begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em, itemsep=0.2em]
        \item Candidate's name and position sought
        \item Statement of intent (maximum 300 words)
        \item Confirmation of eligibility
        \item Endorsement by at least one other member (for nominations of others)
    \end{itemize}
    \item Candidates may withdraw their nomination up to 24 hours before the election.
\end{enumerate}
\\
\textbf{Voting:}
\begin{enumerate}[label=\alph*), leftmargin=2em, itemsep=0.3em]
    \item Elections shall be conducted by secret ballot during the Reformation Meeting.
    
    \item All active members in good standing are eligible to vote.
    
    \item Voting shall be conducted using a secure Google Form or equivalent digital voting system that ensures:
    \begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em, itemsep=0.2em]
        \item One vote per eligible member
        \item Anonymity of the voter
        \item Prevention of duplicate voting
        \item Verifiable vote count
    \end{itemize}
    
    \item The President's vote shall carry the weight of three (3) votes in the popular vote calculation.
    
    \item The form must be reset or refreshed for each ballot to maintain security and prevent tampering.
\end{enumerate}
\\
\textbf{Vote Calculation:}
The outcome of the election shall be determined by a weighted scoring system:

\begin{enumerate}[label=\arabic*. , leftmargin=2em, itemsep=0.3em]
    \item \textbf{Popular Vote (70\%):} The aggregate votes cast by eligible members, with the President's vote counting as three votes. The Google Form shall be the primary mechanism for collecting popular votes, though alternative secure methods may be approved by the Executive Board.
    
    \item \textbf{Club Involvement and Contribution (20\%):} Assessed by the outgoing President based on the candidate's documented performance record throughout their membership tenure, including:
    \begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em, itemsep=0.2em]
        \item Event participation and contribution quality
        \item Operational support role fulfillment
        \item Project participation and outcomes
        \item Initiative and leadership demonstrated
    \end{itemize}
    
    \item \textbf{Attendance Record (10\%):} Calculated from the Secretary's attendance records, covering both meetings and events throughout the candidate's membership.
    
    \item The final score shall be computed as a weighted average of these three components.
    
    \item The candidate with the highest final score for each position shall be declared elected.
\end{enumerate}
\\
\textbf{Tie-Breaking:}
\begin{enumerate}[label=\alph*), leftmargin=2em, itemsep=0.3em]
    \item In the event of a tie in the final score, a runoff election shall be held immediately between the tied candidates.
    \item The runoff shall be decided by a simple majority vote of members present.
    \item If the tie persists after the runoff, the outgoing President shall cast the deciding vote.
\end{enumerate}

\gsection{Transition of Power}

\begin{enumerate}[label=\alph*), leftmargin=2em, itemsep=0.3em]
    \item A formal transition of all responsibilities, documents, and accounts from the outgoing to the incoming officers must be completed within one week (7 days) of the election.
    
    \item The transition process shall include:
    \begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em, itemsep=0.2em]
        \item Handover of all physical Club property (keys, badges, stamp, equipment)
        \item Transfer of all digital account credentials (Outlook, Gmail, social media, Canva)
        \item Transfer of all documents, records, and archives
        \item Transfer of the financial statement and all financial records
        \item Briefing on ongoing projects, pending tasks, and relationships
        \item Introduction to external contacts and stakeholders
    \end{itemize}
    
    \item \textbf{Password Reset Requirement:} All administrative passwords (Outlook, Canva, social media accounts, shared drives) must be officially reset and new credentials handed over within 48 hours of the new board taking office.
    
    \item The new officers shall officially assume their roles at the beginning of the next academic year.
    
    \item Outgoing officers shall remain available for consultation for a period of 30 days after the transition.
    
    \item The outgoing President shall provide a comprehensive handover memo documenting:
    \begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em, itemsep=0.2em]
        \item Status of all ongoing activities
        \item Key relationships and contacts
        \item Lessons learned and recommendations
        \item Individual performance observations for appointment decisions
    \end{itemize}
\end{enumerate}

\gsection{Proposing Amendments}

\begin{enumerate}[label=\alph*), leftmargin=2em, itemsep=0.3em]
    \item Any member may propose an amendment by submitting a written proposal to the Executive Board.
    
    \item The proposal must clearly state:
    \begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em, itemsep=0.2em]
        \item Which article and section are being changed
        \item The exact wording of the proposed change
        \item The rationale for the change
        \item How the change will affect Club operations
    \end{itemize}
    
    \item The Executive Board shall review all proposals and provide their recommendation (support, oppose, or neutral) within 7 days.
\end{enumerate}

\gsection{Review and Distribution}

\begin{enumerate}[label=\alph*), leftmargin=2em, itemsep=0.3em]
    \item The Executive Board reviews the proposal and may suggest modifications.
    
    \item The proposal is shared with all members at least 24 hours before the meeting where it will be voted on.
    
    \item The proposal shall also be posted in the main communication channel to initiate the 7-day Constructive Review period as specified in Article IV, Section 5.
    
    \item The Executive Board provides their recommendation (support, oppose, or neutral) to all members.
\end{enumerate}

\gsection{Voting on Amendments}

\begin{enumerate}[label=\alph*), leftmargin=2em, itemsep=0.3em]
    \item Amendments are voted on at a general meeting with quorum present.
    
    \item The proposer shall have the opportunity to explain their reasoning.
    
    \item Members may discuss the proposal before voting.
    
    \item An amendment passes if it receives a two-thirds (2/3) majority vote of the members present.
    
    \item Abstentions shall not count toward the total vote count.
\end{enumerate}

\gsection{Implementing Amendments}

\begin{enumerate}[label=\alph*), leftmargin=2em, itemsep=0.3em]
    \item Once approved, the Secretary shall update this document within 3 days.
    
    \item The updated document shall be distributed to all members within 7 days.
    
    \item Previous versions shall be archived with notes documenting what changed.
    
    \item The ``Last Amended'' date shall be updated on the Document Information page.
    
    \item Significant changes shall be reported to the Department through the Head of Department.
\end{enumerate}

\gsection{Constitutional Lock}

\begin{enumerate}[label=\alph*), leftmargin=2em, itemsep=0.3em]
    \item Following the adoption or amendment of this Constitution, the immediately succeeding tenure shall remain bound by the Constitution without review or amendment privileges.
    
    \item Constitutional review or amendment may only occur during the leadership transition period of the subsequent eligible tenure.
    
    \item This restriction ensures constitutional stability and prevents frequent disruptive changes across consecutive tenures.
    
    \item \textbf{Emergency Amendment Exception:} In exceptional circumstances requiring immediate amendment (such as conflicts with newly enacted college policies), the Executive Board may call an emergency amendment vote with unanimous Board consent and approval from the Head of Department.
    
    \item The designated constitutional review tenure shall serve as the primary opportunity for members to propose and discuss amendments.
\end{enumerate}

\gsection{Constitutional Authority}

\begin{enumerate}[label=\alph*), leftmargin=2em, itemsep=0.3em]
    \item This Governance Charter is the supreme governing document of the NCE IT Club.
    
    \item If there is any conflict between this document and other Club documents, decisions, or practices, this document shall take precedence.
    
    \item The President's actions and decisions based on the provisions of this document are binding on all members.
    
    \item Members who disagree with the application of any provision may propose an amendment during the next annual review window, but must comply with the current provisions in the interim.
    
    \item No oral agreement, informal understanding, or past practice shall override the written provisions of this document.
    
    \item The Club shall operate in compliance with all applicable college and departmental regulations. In case of conflict between this document and institutional regulations, the institutional regulations shall take precedence, and this document shall be amended to achieve alignment at the earliest opportunity.
\end{enumerate}
