% ============================================
% ARTICLE IV: MEETINGS & DECISION-MAKING
% ============================================
\article{Meetings \& Decision-Making}

\gsection{Types of Meetings}

The Club shall conduct the following types of meetings:

\begin{enumerate}[label=\alph*), leftmargin=2em, itemsep=0.3em]
    \item \textbf{General Meetings:} Regular meetings held for planning, discussion, and decision-making. These may be called by the President, Secretary, or upon written request by at least 50\% of the active members. Meetings conducted during the post-reformation period for recruitment and essential club operations until full membership strength are referred to as \textbf{Interim Meetings}.
    
    \item \textbf{Event Debrief Meetings:} Mandatory meetings held after every major event to review outcomes, identify improvements, and document lessons learned.
    
    \item \textbf{Reformation Meeting:} The annual meeting for the election of new officers and appointment of designated roles, conducted in accordance with Article VIII.
    
    \item \textbf{Flash Meetings (Emergency Meetings):} Urgent meetings called by the President for sudden, time-sensitive matters. These may be called with shorter notice than standard general meetings.
\end{enumerate}

\gsection{Meeting Procedures}

\textbf{Scheduling and Notice:}
\begin{enumerate}[label=\alph*), leftmargin=2em, itemsep=0.3em]
    \item The President or Secretary may call a general meeting.
    \item A general meeting must also be called if requested in writing by at least 50\% of the members.
    \item The Secretary shall provide members with advance notice of at least 24--48 hours for all scheduled general meetings.
    \item The notice shall include the meeting agenda, date, time, and location.
    \item \textbf{Flash Meeting Exception:} The President may call a Flash Meeting with as little as 6 hours notice for urgent matters. The President must document the reason for the shortened notice and share it with all members.
    \item \textbf{Event Debrief Meeting Exception:} Event Debrief Meetings are conducted immediately after the completion of an event without prior notice or formal agenda.
\end{enumerate}
\\
\textbf{Quorum:}
\begin{enumerate}[label=\alph*), leftmargin=2em, itemsep=0.3em]
    \item A quorum, consisting of more than half (50\% + 1) of the total active membership, is required to conduct official business.
    \item No binding decisions may be made in the absence of a quorum.
    \item If a quorum is not met within 30 minutes of the scheduled start time, the meeting may be adjourned and rescheduled.
\end{enumerate}
\\
\textbf{Minutes:}
\begin{enumerate}[label=\alph*), leftmargin=2em, itemsep=0.3em]
    \item The Secretary shall record and maintain minutes for all official meetings.
    \item Minutes shall include: date, time, attendees, agenda items, decisions made, votes taken, and action items.
    \item Draft minutes shall be circulated to all members within 48 hours of the meeting.
    \item Minutes shall be approved at the next scheduled meeting or by silent consent.
\end{enumerate}
\\
\textbf{Online Meetings:}
\begin{enumerate}[label=\alph*), leftmargin=2em, itemsep=0.3em]
    \item General Meetings and Flash Meetings may be conducted in an online format when physical meetings are not feasible.
    \item Event Debrief Meetings and Reformation Meetings are preferably conducted in-person; however, they may be held online in exceptional circumstances as determined by the President or Secretary.
    \item For online meetings, the meeting link shall be shared by the Secretary in the official communication channel at least 5 minutes prior to the scheduled start time.
    \item Members are expected to join online meetings with video enabled unless granted an exemption by the President or Secretary due to valid technical or personal constraints.
    \item Members shall maintain basic professional conduct during online meetings, including appropriate naming, muting when not speaking, and active participation.
\end{enumerate}


\gsection{Event Debriefings}

\begin{enumerate}[label=\alph*), leftmargin=2em, itemsep=0.3em]
    \item A formal debriefing meeting is mandatory after the conclusion of each major Club event.
    \item For multi-day events, informal daily reviews may be conducted to address immediate operational needs, with a formal debrief held after the event's conclusion.
    \item The purpose of the debrief is to evaluate the event's success, discuss challenges, and document lessons learned for future planning.
    \item The Event Manager shall present a summary report including:
    \begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em, itemsep=0.2em]
        \item Event objectives and whether they were achieved
        \item Attendance and participation statistics
        \item Budget compliance and financial summary
        \item Operational challenges encountered
        \item Recommendations for future similar events
    \end{itemize}
    \item Debrief findings shall be archived by the Secretary for future reference.
\end{enumerate}

\gsection{Voting Procedures}

\textbf{General Voting:}
\begin{enumerate}[label=\alph*), leftmargin=2em, itemsep=0.3em]
    \item Decisions shall be made by a simple majority vote of the members present, provided a quorum is met.
    \item Each member present shall have one vote, except the President who may cast additional votes as specified elsewhere in this document.
    \item In the event of a tie, the President may:
    \begin{enumerate}[label=\roman*. , leftmargin=2em, itemsep=0.2em]
        \item Cast a deciding vote, or
        \item Call for a revote, or
        \item Table the matter for a future meeting.
    \end{enumerate}
\end{enumerate}
\\
\textbf{Secret Ballot:}
\begin{enumerate}[label=\alph*), leftmargin=2em, itemsep=0.3em]
    \item Voting on sensitive matters may be conducted by secret ballot if requested by at least 25\% of the members present or at the discretion of the Executive Board.
    \item Secret ballots shall be administered by the Secretary or a neutral party designated by the President.
    \item Results shall be announced immediately after counting is complete.
\end{enumerate}
\\
\gsection{Constructive Review and Silent Consent Rule}

\begin{enumerate}[label=\alph*), leftmargin=2em, itemsep=0.3em]
    \item To ensure efficient decision-making while respecting member input, the Club adopts the following Constructive Review procedure for major decisions, constitutional amendments, and governance drafts.
    
    \item \textbf{Posting Period:} When a governance draft, amendment proposal, or major decision is posted in the main communication channel, members shall have seven (7) consecutive days to review and submit written objections, suggestions, or recommendations.
    
    \item \textbf{Written Objection Requirement:} Only written objections submitted within the 7-day period shall be considered valid. Verbal expressions of disagreement, reactions, or informal comments shall not constitute formal objections.
    
    \item \textbf{Collective Discussion:} After the 7-day review period, all submitted objections and suggestions shall be compiled and presented at the next general meeting for collective discussion and voting.
    
    \item \textbf{Silent Consent Binding Agreement:} Any provision that receives no written objection within the 7-day period shall be deemed approved by silent consent and shall constitute a binding collective agreement by all members. No further challenge to such provisions shall be entertained.
    
    \item \textbf{Amendment of Objected Provisions:} Only those provisions that received written objections shall be subject to amendment discussion and potential revision.
    
    \item This rule applies to:
    \begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em, itemsep=0.2em]
        \item Amendments to this Governance Charter
        \item Major policy changes
        \item Significant operational decisions affecting all members
        \item Election procedure modifications
    \end{itemize}
\end{enumerate}

\gsection{Attendance Policy}

\begin{enumerate}[label=\alph*), leftmargin=2em, itemsep=0.3em]
    \item Members are expected to attend all scheduled meetings and events.
    \item In case of absence, members must provide advance notice, at least 30 minutes prior to the meeting to the President or Secretary with a valid reason.
    \item \textbf{Two-Meeting Warning Threshold:} Missing two consecutive scheduled meetings or official events without an approved, documented reason submitted to the President or Secretary shall automatically trigger a Stage 1 (Verbal Warning) under Article VII.
    \item \textbf{Repeated Absences:} A pattern of repeated unexcused absences (three or more in a semester) shall result in a review of membership status and may lead to further disciplinary action.
    \item Valid reasons for absence may include academic, health, personal, or technical constraints, including but not limited to examinations, documented illness, emergencies, or connectivity/power issues, subject to review by the Executive Board.
\end{enumerate}
