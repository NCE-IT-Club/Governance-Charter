\article{Organizational Structure \& Leadership}

\gsection{Executive Board}

The Club is led by an Executive Board consisting of three officers:
\begin{enumerate}[label=\arabic*. , leftmargin=2em, itemsep=0.3em]
    \item President
    \item Secretary
    \item Treasurer
\end{enumerate}
\\
These positions are elected annually through Reformation Process (Article VIII, Section 4).
\\
\textbf{Executive Board Authority:}
\begin{enumerate}[label=\alph*), leftmargin=2em, itemsep=0.3em]
    \item The Executive Board collectively holds final decision-making authority over all Club matters.
    \item The President shall serve as the Chief Executive Officer of the Club, presiding over all meetings and representing the Club externally.
    \item The Secretary shall serve as the Chief Administrative Officer, maintaining all records, minutes, and official correspondence.
    \item The Treasurer shall serve as the Chief Financial Officer, managing all Club funds and financial records.
\end{enumerate}

\gsection{Designated Officer Roles}

In addition to the Executive Board, the Club shall have designated officer roles to manage specific operational areas. These positions are appointed by the incoming Executive Board within 48 hours of the Reformation Meeting.

\textbf{Designated Roles:}
\begin{enumerate}[label=\arabic*. , leftmargin=2em, itemsep=0.3em]
    \item Event Manager
    \item Social Media Manager
    \item Design Lead
    \item Public Relations Officer
    \item Project Lead
\end{enumerate}
\\
\textbf{Appointment Process:}
\begin{enumerate}[label=\alph*), leftmargin=2em, itemsep=0.3em]
    \item These positions are \textbf{not} subject to popular election. They are strictly appointed by the incoming Executive Trio through a closed merit-based review.
    \item Appointment decisions shall be based on three weighted metrics:
    \begin{enumerate}[label=\arabic*. , leftmargin=2em, itemsep=0.2em]
        \item \textbf{Demonstrated Interest (40\%):} The candidate's expressed enthusiasm, initiative in seeking the role, and alignment with the role's requirements.
        \item \textbf{Technical Capability (35\%):} Relevant skills, prior experience, and demonstrated competence in the functional area.
        \item \textbf{Prior Performance Score (25\%):} Documented performance in Club activities, operational support, and event contribution during their membership tenure.
    \end{enumerate}
    \item The outgoing Executive Board shall provide performance records and observations to assist the incoming Board in making informed appointment decisions.
    \item Members who do not hold a designated officer role shall serve as general members and contribute through the operational support roles outlined in Article II.
\end{enumerate}

\gsection{Roles and Responsibilities Handbook}

\begin{enumerate}[label=\alph*), leftmargin=2em, itemsep=0.3em]
    \item Detailed duties, operational expectations, authorities, and responsibilities of all Club positions shall be maintained in a separate official document titled the \textbf{``Roles and Responsibilities Handbook.''}
    
    \item The Handbook shall serve as the primary operational reference for all officers holding designated responsibilities within the Club.
    
    \item All officers are required to perform their assigned duties in accordance with the provisions outlined in the Handbook.
    
    \item The Executive Board may revise or expand the Handbook as operational needs evolve, provided such revisions do not conflict with this Governance Charter.
\end{enumerate}

\gsection{Project Development Unit (PDU)}

The Club shall maintain a standing Project Development Unit (PDU) as its technical delivery and external project representation arm.

\begin{enumerate}[label=\alph*), leftmargin=2em, itemsep=0.3em]
    \item The PDU shall be headed by the Project Lead and may include members selected by the Project Lead based on interest, technical ability, availability, and project needs, subject to Executive Board oversight.
    \item The PDU's core mandate shall include developing Club projects, preparing project demonstrations, and representing the Club at external technical events such as hackathons, ideathons, competitions, exhibitions, and similar opportunities.
    \item The PDU shall remain on active standby during the regular academic year and may be mobilized whenever project or external event opportunities arise. During official college examination periods, its operational obligations may be reduced or suspended in accordance with Article VI.
    \item The Project Lead shall maintain a readiness roster and coordinate with the Executive Board to deploy the PDU for project work and external event participation.
    \item The PDU is a standing Club function, not an ad hoc group, and its membership may be adjusted as project needs change.
\end{enumerate}
