\section*{Preamble}
\sectionmark{Preamble}

We, the members of the NCE IT Club, establish this Governance Charter to formalize our organization and create a clear framework for our operations and governance.

The NCE IT Club has been operating at the National College of Engineering for several years, bringing together students passionate about technology and innovation. This document represents our commitment to creating a structured, transparent, and effective organization.

We recognize that information technology plays a crucial role in modern society, and we are committed to helping our fellow students develop both technical and professional skills that will serve them throughout their careers.

This Governance Charter serves as the official governing document of the NCE IT Club. It defines:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=2em, itemsep=0.3em]
    \item Our organizational structure and leadership roles
    \item How we make decisions and conduct business
    \item The rights and responsibilities of our members
    \item Our standards of conduct and accountability
\end{itemize}

Through this document, we aim to:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=2em, itemsep=0.3em]
    \item Build technical knowledge and practical skills among students
    \item Create a supportive community of technology enthusiasts
    \item Connect academic learning with real-world industry practice
    \item Organize meaningful events and projects for our college community
    \item Maintain high standards of professionalism and integrity
\end{itemize}

Formally enacted this 15th day of Jestha, 2082 B.S. (May 28, 2025 A.D.), under the joint leadership of President Shlok Pandey and Er.~Suroj Burlakoti, Head of the Department of Electronics and Computer Engineering.
